About Us

Welcome to Komu.Inn — where comfort meets modern elegance. Located in the heart of Batam, our hotel offers a perfect blend of warm hospitality and contemporary amenities, ideal for both leisure and business travelers.

Each room is thoughtfully designed with plush bedding, high-speed Wi-Fi, and cozy interiors that ensure a restful stay. Guests can also enjoy our on-site dining, wellness facilities, and convenient access to major city attractions.

Whether you're here for vacation, a romantic escape, or a business trip, Komu.Inn is dedicated to making your stay memorable — experience comfort like never before.
